# Code of Conduct

videocraft follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its full text is the code of conduct for this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you
act on the project's behalf.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, critique the work and not the person, and make it easy for
people who are new to Minecraft modding or to video to take part.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/alexandros-lekkas), or open a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/alexandros-lekkas/videocraft/security/advisories/new) marked "conduct" if you
need a private channel. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer decides on consequences following the
Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
