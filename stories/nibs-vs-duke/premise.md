# Nibs vs Duke

## Logline
Nibs, a one-horned goat who is sure it is the best jumper in the valley, struts down the market lane bragging to us,
until the Talent Night poster reminds it who has won three years running: Duke, a smug snow fox. Nibs challenges
Duke to a jump-off; Duke yawns and goes to sleep.

## Tone
Cocky narrator comedy: a loud, self-adoring hero talking straight to the audience, a deadpan rival who never takes
the bait. Fast, bright, a little absurd; the jokes are in the cutting, the looks to the lens and the animals' own
business (an egg for applause, a yawn for an answer).

## Audience
Minecraft comedy viewers on YouTube, 8 to 30; watched cold, with the sound on (voice and captions) or off (captions).

## Format
short, 16:9 (1920x1080, 60 fps), 71.75 s measured (shots 1-30 and two dips to black) plus a 2 s end card:
73.75 s in all, 30 shots, median about 2.6 s, no shot over 7.5 s.

## Reference form
The PinkSheep-style benchmark (docs/benchmarks/pinksheep-style.md): the reference cold open's structure, shot count,
relative lengths, camera language (lead tracks about half the screen time, statics a quarter, a face cut-in, inserts,
graphic cards), a framed flashback, a six-shot brag montage and dips to black, matched shot for shot. Only the form:
the characters, lines, jokes, sets, music and sounds are ours.

## Characters
- **Nibs**: a slate-blue-grey goat with one horn (a vanilla goat without its left horn). Wants everyone to admit it
  is the best jumper in the valley.
- **Duke**: a snow fox (the white vanilla variant), three-time Talent Night champion. Wants nothing; already has it.
- **The audience**: four chickens on a fence rail. Want nothing either. One lays an egg.

## The story in five beats
- Setup: Nibs struts down Market Lane telling us how magnificent it is (the horn, the snacks).
- Escalation: the notice board: Talent Night, champion three years running, Duke. Again.
- Turn: last year's show, framed like a memory: Duke's note-block solo, the trophy, the chickens' only applause is one egg.
- Payoff: back on the lane Nibs decides it is at least six times more goat than that fox and screams a challenge at the sky.
- Button: Duke's card; then Duke himself, on black, hears it, yawns and lies down to sleep.

## Rules
- Original characters, lines, jokes, sets, music and sounds; nothing from the reference video's channel (no sheep,
  no pink, no moustache, no rival character, no audio).
- Everything staged and filmed in Minecraft: sets of vanilla blocks, mobs as actors, cards and text drawn in game.
- Nibs' voice is a synthetic narrator voice; each Nibs line also starts on a goat bleat, each Duke line on a fox yip.
