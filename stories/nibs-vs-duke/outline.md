# Nibs vs Duke: outline

Target: 71.75 s measured (30 shots and two dips to black) plus a 2 s end card; the benchmark's form shot for shot
(docs/benchmarks/pinksheep-style.md, "Shot list"). Shot numbers below are the benchmark's.

## 1. The strut (setup, ~12 s, shots 1-4)
Market Lane, late afternoon. Nibs struts towards a backwards-travelling wide lens, introducing itself to us: the
best jumper in the whole valley. A face cut-in on "horn" sells the one-horned look as a feature. Purpose: who Nibs
is (loud, vain, a vlogger to its bones) and the world (a market lane that is its runway).

## 2. Snacks (escalation, ~2.5 s, shot 5)
The first look to the lens: Nibs stops, turns its head to us, deadpan, on "snacks". Purpose: the trivial errand,
told as an epic; establishes the look to the lens as Nibs' tell.

## 3. The notice board (turn, ~10 s, shots 6-9)
Nibs passes the well, slows at the notice board; a straight-on insert of the poster: Talent Night, champion three
years running, Duke. "Duke. Again." Nibs stares, walks out of frame; dip to black and silence. Purpose: the rival
and the wound, and the doorway into the memory.

## 4. Last year's show (the flashback, ~25 s, shots 10-18)
The barn at night, framed like a memory (amber border, sepia). Duke sits centre stage, trots to the note blocks,
plays the first note with its head, "Hit it.", the note blocks carry the solo on their own, a slow push-in on one
block. Wide and still: Duke bows, picks up the trophy and waits for applause; nothing. Cut to a chicken facing the
lens: it lays an egg. "A standing ovation." Purpose: show why Duke wins, and puncture it; setup wide, punchline close.

## 5. Six times more goat (payoff, ~14 s, shots 19-28)
Back on the lane, harder music. Nibs, head down, lifts it with resolve: "That fox can't even jump." Round the corner,
"I'm at least…" and the six-shot brag montage, one word a shot: SIX TIMES MORE GOAT THAN HIM. Walking on, very
pleased; at the open end of the lane it stops, turns to the lens, lowers its head to ram, lifts it and screams the
challenge at the sky. Dip to black on the scream. Purpose: the challenge, as big as Nibs can make it.

## 6. Duke's answer (button, ~6 s, shots 29-30 and the end card)
Duke's card on black: "DUKE · 3× champion", a slow push. Then Duke himself, small on black, hears it, yawns and lies
down to sleep: "zzz". End card: NIBS vs DUKE · The Jump-Off. Purpose: the button; the rival does not even play.
