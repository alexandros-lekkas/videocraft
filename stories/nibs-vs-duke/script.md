# Nibs vs Duke: script

Shot numbers are the benchmark's (docs/benchmarks/pinksheep-style.md, "Shot list"); lengths are its targets.
NIBS (VO) is Nibs' narration over the shot; every Nibs line starts on a goat bleat, every Duke line on a fox yip.
`*` marks the key word: the cut lands on it, and the caption highlights it.

## Scene 1: market-lane, late afternoon

> 1 (6.2 s). Lead track, wide lens, the camera a block up and pitched down a little. Nibs struts towards us up the
> curving lane, head high, stalls on both sides, lanterns, a cart.

[SOUND: music bed starts on frame 1, upbeat and light; goat footsteps]

NIBS (VO): Hey. It's me. *Nibs.
NIBS (VO): Best jumper in the whole valley.

> 2 (1.2 s). Face cut-in: Nibs' face and its single horn fill the frame; it holds still and blinks once; a punch-in
> from 1.0× to 1.15× over the shot.

NIBS (VO): This *horn.

[SOUND: a note-block bell "ding" on "horn"]

> 3 (1.7 s). Lead track. Nibs crosses a small plank bridge over a stream.

NIBS (VO): One's plenty.

[SOUND: footsteps change to wood]

> 4 (3.1 s). Lead track, a new stretch: Nibs walks past a stall with a striped awning.

NIBS (VO): Anyway. I was heading to the *market...

> 5 (2.4 s). Lead track, closer: Nibs at about half the frame height, by the well. It stops and turns its head to
> the lens, deadpan.

NIBS (VO): ...for *snacks.

[SOUND: one beat of music dropout as it looks at the lens]

> 6 (4.7 s). Lead track. Nibs walks on past the well, a lantern and flower pots.

NIBS (VO): Then I walked past the *notice board.

> 7 (2.8 s). Lead track, a jump cut to a new angle. Nibs slows down, its head turning right as the notice board
> edges into frame on the right.

NIBS (VO): And I saw it.

[SOUND: the bed thins to drums]

> 8 (2.3 s). Insert, full frame and straight on: the poster on the notice board, a pixel-art white fox with a
> trophy.

[CARD: "TALENT NIGHT · Champion 3 years running: DUKE" (the poster's own text, in the world)]

NIBS (VO): *Duke. Again.

[SOUND: a low note-block "dun" on "Duke"]

> 9 (2.6 s, then 0.8 s of black). Lead track: Nibs stares at the board, then walks out of frame.

NIBS (VO): I remember last year's show...

[DIP TO BLACK over the last 0.4 s; the bed fades out with the picture; 0.8 s of silence]

## Scene 2: barn-stage, night (the flashback, framed: amber border, sepia grade, shots 10-18)

> 10 (0.6 s). Static, fading up from black: Duke sits centre stage, chin up, lanterns behind.

[SOUND: barn ambience: crickets, a creak]

> 11 (1.1 s). Static, high and wide: Duke trots across the stage to the note blocks; the chickens on the rail.

[SOUND: fox footsteps on wood]

> 12 (1.2 s). Insert, close: Duke dips its head to the first note block, which plays on that frame.

[SOUND: the first note of Duke's solo]

> 13 (2.7 s). Static, medium, the trophy big in the foreground: Duke sits beside it, smug.

DUKE: Hit it.

[SOUND: the solo starts properly: an original note-block melody, about 120 bpm]

> 14 (4.4 s). Static, medium on the note-block row: the blocks play on their own, note particles rising from each
> in time.

[SOUND: the melody, in the world and positional]

> 15 (4.3 s). Insert with a slow push-in (1.0× to 1.2×): close on one note block as its particles rise.

[SOUND: the melody's big phrase]

> 16 (5.3 s). Static, high and wide; the chickens big in the foreground, Duke small on stage. Duke finishes, bows
> (a crouch), picks up the trophy in its mouth and waits for applause. The chickens do nothing.

DUKE: Thank you. Thank *you.

[SOUND: the melody ends; 1.5 s of silence but for the crickets]

> 17 (2.7 s). Static, medium close-up of one chicken facing the lens. It blinks, then lays an egg; the egg pops out
> beside it. Hold 0.5 s after it.

[SOUND: the egg plop, then one cluck]

> 18 (2.6 s). Static, wide: Duke on stage, the trophy in its mouth, tail up, satisfied. The flashback frame ends
> with the shot.

DUKE: A *standing ovation.

[SOUND: a slow yip; the ambience fades]

## Scene 3: market-lane, late afternoon

> 19 (4.5 s). Lead track, a new stretch: Nibs walks with its head down, then lifts it with resolve.

NIBS (VO): That fox can't even *jump.

[SOUND: the bed returns, harder: drums in]

> 20 (1.3 s). Lead track, the camera swinging round as Nibs takes a corner.

NIBS (VO): I'm at least...

> 21-26 (0.5, 0.25, 0.5, 0.35, 0.5, 0.25 s). The brag montage: lead track, medium close-up, Nibs strutting at the
> lens in six places: in front of a stall, by the lanterns, at the well, by the flower pots, on the bridge (a small
> hop at the lens), by the cart. One word a shot; each shot starts on a kick-drum hit.

NIBS (VO): *SIX
NIBS (VO): *TIMES
NIBS (VO): *MORE
NIBS (VO): *GOAT
NIBS (VO): *THAN
NIBS (VO): *HIM

[SOUND: a kick-drum hit on each cut; a hop sound on shot 25]

> 27 (1.6 s). Lead track, wide: Nibs walks on, very pleased.

NIBS (VO): So today...

> 28 (4.6 s, then 0.6 s of black). Lead track, a jump cut to the open end of the lane, the valley behind. Nibs walks,
> stops, turns to the lens (the camera swings round with it), lowers its head as if to ram, then lifts it and
> screams at the sky.

NIBS (VO): ...I *challenge Duke to a jump-off!

[SOUND: the screaming goat's scream, full]
[DIP TO BLACK on the scream; cut to silence]

## Scene 4: black-stage

> 29 (3.0 s). Graphic card: Duke's own model in a front three-quarter pose, centred on black, fading up; a slow
> 1.0× to 1.05× push.

[CARD: "DUKE · 3× champion"]

[SOUND: a note-block sting]

> 30 (1.1 s). Duke, small and centred on black under one light, hears it, yawns and lies down to sleep.

DUKE: *zzz

[SOUND: a tiny fox snore; the music's button]

> End card (2.0 s): the title on black.

[CARD: "NIBS vs DUKE · The Jump-Off"]

[SOUND: the bed's final hit, then out]
